# quality

* [Full Quality, Waste Removed](/quality/full-quality-no-waste.md) - The non-functional requirement that the camera is used at full chosen quality and CPU is saved only by removing waste.
* [Installability](/quality/installability.md) - The non-functional requirement that installation and launch feel like a normal desktop application.
* [Low-Latency Tracking Loop](/quality/low-latency-loop.md) - The non-functional requirement that perception and decoding stay inside the loop's time budget.
* [Privacy First](/quality/privacy-first.md) - The non-functional requirement that privacy never leaks the room image or motion to callers.
* [Smooth, Calm Tracking](/quality/smooth-tracking.md) - The non-functional requirement that the camera moves rarely and smoothly, like a calm operator.
