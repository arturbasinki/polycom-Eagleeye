# use-cases

* [Calibrate the Camera](/use-cases/calibrate-camera.md) - The use case of measuring one camera unit and recording the result as a configuration override.
* [Control a Running Instance](/use-cases/control-running-instance.md) - The use case of driving the running application through the UNIX socket from CLI or tray.
* [Correct the Light](/use-cases/correct-light.md) - The use case of measuring the face once and applying a scene-specific tone curve.
* [Enable Privacy Mode](/use-cases/enable-privacy.md) - The use case of hiding the room and pointing the lens down, then restoring the previous pose.
* [Enable/Disable Auto-Tracking](/use-cases/enable-tracking.md) - The use case of starting and stopping automatic tracking, including the startup search.
* [Replay a Session](/use-cases/replay-session.md) - The use case of recording tracking sessions and replaying them offline for diagnosis and tuning.
* [Save/Recall a Preset](/use-cases/save-recall-preset.md) - The use case of storing and recalling full camera poses, including the home pose.
* [Select Person to Follow](/use-cases/select-person.md) - The use case where the operator picks one person for the camera to follow.
* [Switch the UI Language](/use-cases/switch-language.md) - The use case of changing the interface language at runtime.
