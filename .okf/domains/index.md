# domains

* [Application Lifecycle and Control](/domains/application-lifecycle-and-control.md) - The domain of process lifecycle, background operation, remote control, single-instance locking and installation.
* [Automatic Tracking](/domains/automatic-tracking.md) - The core subdomain: detect people, keep the chosen person framed, and recover when they are lost.
* [Image Tuning and Light Correction](/domains/image-tuning-and-light-correction.md) - The subdomain of image controls and the one-shot face-driven tone-curve light correction.
* [Internationalization](/domains/internationalization.md) - The subdomain of runtime translation catalogs, language detection and localized messages.
* [Person Identity](/domains/person-identity.md) - The supporting subdomain that numbers people across frames and tracks the operator's chosen person.
* [PTZ Camera Control](/domains/ptz-camera-control.md) - Driving the motorised head, optics and image controls of the camera, and saving/recalling full camera poses.
* [Virtual Camera and Privacy](/domains/virtual-camera-and-privacy.md) - The subdomain that publishes the framed image on a virtual camera and provides privacy mode and the placeholder slate.
