# data-flows

* [Frame to Command](/data-flows/frame-to-command.md) - The end-to-end data flow from an MJPEG frame through pose, world angles and filtering to PTZ commands.
* [Light Correction Flow](/data-flows/light-correction.md) - The data flow from frame and face to the tone LUT and its application in the virtual camera.
* [Settings and Presets Flow](/data-flows/settings-and-presets.md) - How settings and presets are applied to live objects and persisted atomically.
* [Virtual Camera Output](/data-flows/virtual-camera-output.md) - The data flow that turns camera frames or slates into an I420 virtual-camera stream at the camera's size.
